# Favicon options

Created with the built-in GPT Image Gen tool. The **wave** design is installed. Other options remain available as real assets, with no Next.js artwork.

| Option           | PNG                                   | ICO                                   |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Wave (installed) | `public/favicon-options/wave.png`     | `public/favicon-options/wave.ico`     |
| Pool lanes       | `public/favicon-options/lanes.png`    | `public/favicon-options/lanes.ico`    |
| Calendar + wave  | `public/favicon-options/calendar.png` | `public/favicon-options/calendar.ico` |

PNG options are 512×512. Each ICO contains 16, 32, 48 and 64px PNG frames. The installed Next metadata files are `src/app/favicon.ico`, `src/app/icon.png` (512px) and `src/app/apple-icon.png` (180px). The header uses the generated wave mark too. Sharp was used only to package and resize the generated artwork, and Node buffers packaged the ICO directory.

## Generation prompt set

All three briefs used `logo-brand`, required one actual production favicon, a centered composition readable at 16px, and prohibited lettering, mockups, shadows, texture and tiny details.

### Wave

Create one finished favicon app icon for Bazeni Zagreb, a refined Zagreb public swimming calendar. A solid deep navy #102343 rounded square fills almost the whole image. Center a bold turquoise #00b8aa symbol of TWO horizontal flowing water waves, equal thick strokes, balanced continuous curves. Flat clean graphic, sharp high-contrast edges, no outline, gradients, shadows, texture, 3D, lettering, border or tiny details. Transparent outside the tile. Actual production artwork, not a presentation board.

### Pool lanes

Create one finished app favicon: a vivid turquoise #00a99b rounded tile with an extremely simple white swimming-pool lane symbol seen from above. Two parallel thick vertical lane lines end in bold T shapes, with one short white wave near the upper part. Swiss graphic design, optical balance, recognizable at 16px. No words, numbers, gradients, shadows, texture, 3D, border, tiny details or mockup. Transparent outside the tile.

Final Image Gen edit: keep the two white T-shaped lanes and white wave. Replace the tile background with solid full-bleed teal #009c8d reaching all edges. No rounded outer tile, border, transparency, holes, uneven shade, blotches, texture, shadow, gradient, specks or artifacts. Every background pixel uses the same flat color. Keep the layout and no lettering.

### Calendar + wave

Create one finished app favicon: a pale aqua #e0f5ef rounded tile, with a bold navy #102343 calendar outline, two chunky binding tabs, and one turquoise #00a99b water wave instead of dates. Thick lines, simple flat geometry, Scandinavian utility app styling and generous negative space. Readable at 16px. No text, numbers, gradients, shadows, texture, 3D, extra objects or mockup. Transparent outside the tile.

Final Image Gen edit: keep the navy calendar outline and teal wave. Replace the tile background with solid full-bleed pale aqua #e0f5ef reaching all edges. No rounded outer tile, border, transparency, holes, uneven shade, blotches, texture, shadow, gradient, specks or artifacts. Every background pixel uses the same flat color. Keep the layout and no lettering.
